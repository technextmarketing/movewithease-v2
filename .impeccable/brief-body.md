# Surface brief — Move with Ease website v2 (index.html and every page that shares its shell)

Scope: the whole 25-page static site; mode **Persuade** on the home and service pages, **Read** on terms/resources.
Audience: adults in west Kent (and online) living with persistent pain, anxiety or stress, usually on a phone;
returning clients who want the booking link in two taps; gift buyers. Job: understand what Caroline offers,
whether it fits, what it costs, where, and start (free discovery call, booking, or enquiry). Proof: Caroline's
own qualifications and lived story, five published client reviews, her videos, the clinic photographs.
Constraints (user brief, 13 Sep 2026): sleek, simple, informative; header and section format from
technextmarketing.github.io/technext-website-v2 but simpler and not techy; body-therapy core like
technextmarketing.github.io/tre-singapore; yellow main, green secondary, white ground, no cream; fonts as
TechNext (Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter); built with the impeccable skill. All copy verbatim from the live site.

Interview: this run is unattended (harness reports the user cannot answer mid-task). The brief above is
precise enough to settle the visual world, so the structured question was substituted by the brief itself;
the roll below was acknowledged and built without a decision page. Disclosed to the user at finish.
Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine), stated not asked.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site breathes. One slow breath — four seconds in, six seconds out — is the only motion on the
page and the rhythm of its sections: a dense, useful passage, then a quiet one. It refuses the wellness
category's default (soft cream, serif, lavender, a stock woman on a mat) and the tech-agency default it
borrows its shell from (blue, gradients, glass, stats).

OWN-WORLD: Pure white ground. Logo yellow (#eeb500) owns whole fields — the breathing sun disc behind the
hero photo, the primary button, the closing invitation band, one highlight rule — always carrying dark
green ink, never white text. Logo green (#3e7d78, deepened to #2f6560 for text) carries headings, links,
the nav's active state and the check marks; pale green (#f3f8f7) is the only tinted band. Plus Jakarta Sans
700–800 display with tight tracking, Inter 400–600 text, tabular numerals for every price. Cards are white
with a 1px green-grey line and a soft offset shadow, 14px radius; buttons 12px radius; chips are pills.
Icons are authored 2px-stroke SVG in one weight (arrow, check, chevron, play, pin, mail, menu, close).
With the copy removed you would still see: white page, one big yellow circle, green type, no gradients.

STORY: Within one viewport the visitor knows this is one practitioner (Caroline), that she helps with
persistent pain, anxiety and stress, where (Laddingford, Tunbridge Wells, Paddock Wood, online) and how to
start (free discovery call). The therapy grid answers "what and how much"; Caroline's photo and story answer
"who"; reviews answer "does it work"; the yellow band closes with the invitation. Every page ends one tap
from booking and one tap from the enquiry form.

FIRST VIEWPORT (home, 1440 wide): sticky 72px white header — emblem + "Move with Ease" left, centred nav
(Home · Chronic Pain Recovery · Therapies ▾ · Classes & Workshops ▾ · About ▾ · Contact), yellow "Book a free
call" right. Below, a two-column hero on white: left column (55%) carries the h1 "Support for pain, anxiety,
stress and greater wellbeing" at ~3.6rem, the verbatim lead, two buttons (yellow primary "Book a free discovery
call", ghost "Therapies & prices"), then four quiet place chips. Right column (45%): the breathing sun — a
yellow disc ~440px that scales 1.00→1.06 over 4s and back over 6s, forever, with the garden portrait of
Caroline sitting over its lower-right edge in a rounded frame, and a small caption beneath that alternates
"Breathe in" / "Breathe out" in time. Under the hero, a single-row "where and when" strip. Phones: hero stacks,
the disc shrinks behind the photo, nav becomes a right-hand drawer.

FORM: Candidate 7 of 7 on my ordered grounded list ("the breathing page"; 1 garden-clinic sunlight, 2 NHS
patient leaflet, 3 studio timetable board, 4 physiotherapy pathway diagram, 5 botanical field guide, 6 sunlit
price list, 7 the breathing page). Seed key **fa1f1b03**, scope direction, mode persuade. Challenger
verdicts: Dong Ho shell-ground print — declined (kept: flat separate colour areas, no gradients anywhere);
Toyism collective plate — declined (kept: one fixed small symbol set in one stroke weight); cracktro scroller
queue — declined (kept: enclose nothing unnecessarily — rules and space before boxes, never nested cards);
collider event display — declined (kept: one compositional law, the concentric disc rules the hero);
sewing pattern envelope — competitive on clarity (kept: the recommended price option renders solid, the
others quiet); ikebana ma arrangement — competitive on identification (kept: active emptiness, asymmetric
hero, generous section gaps). The user-pinned brief (TechNext shell, TRE core, yellow/green/white, fonts)
beats the roll field by field; the assignment binds the motion grammar, pacing and the hero's signature object.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict,
DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
