Terms and Conditions

Contract

Pay for 34 classes per year but receive 2 free classes. That's 36 Move with Ease classes that you can use in your subscription year! You can choose to attend any Move with Ease group classes. The price per class that you pay as a member is a reduced rate compared to pay-as-you-go. To help you your payments will be spread out over 12 months.

Cancel your membership at any time

Be reassured that you can cancel your membership at any time by sending written notice to info@movewithease.org.uk. Please allow at least 5 working days to process the cancellation before your next payment is due. If you have used more class credits than you’ve paid for, you will need to pay the difference at the reduced membership rate of £10 per class before your subscription is officially cancelled.

Booking a Class

Once you purchase a Move with Ease classes membership, you’ll receive a unique booking code that allows you to book available classes up to 3 months in advance. Your code is valid for 36 classes within a one-year period. Make sure to use your code when booking on the Move with Ease website to avoid paying the pay-as-you-go rate. If spaces are still available, you can book a class up to 1 hour before its start time.

Need to cancel a class booking?

You can cancel a class up to 24 hours before it starts. If you cancel after this time, your class credit will not be reimbursed.

Used all your class credits and need more?

If you have used your 36 credits early but still have weeks left in your subscription year? No problem! You can purchase additional class credits at the membership discounted rate of £10 per class to continue enjoying your classes for the remainder of your subscription year.

Refunds

As an independent small business, I am unable to offer refunds for any services purchased. However, if I need to cancel a class, the class credit will be fully reimbursed to your account for future use. If your subscription year is over and you have credits remaining these will not be carried over into another subscription year.

Members booking

Use the button below to book your Thursday Yoga & Fascia Movement classes. Remember to enter your unique membership code when booking — no additional payment will be required.

Book Yoga & Fascia Movement classes Book Live with Ease (members)

If you’d like to book several classes at once, the easiest way is to use the Schedule link in the order receipt you received when you purchased your membership. This allows you to select and book multiple class dates using your membership code.

You can also create a client account when booking, making it easier to view and manage your upcoming and previous class bookings.

If you need any help booking your classes, please email Caroline at info@movewithease.org.uk.

Step-by-Step Booking Guide

  1. Once you have purchased your membership you’ll have received an email. This confirmation email has a unique certificate code & a Schedule link. Click on the Schedule link to book your class.
  2. You’ll be directed to the booking page. Choose the class that you’d like to book.
  3. Choose the date and time that you’d like to attend. You can book 1 class by choosing ‘Select and continue’ or multiple classes by choosing ‘Select and add another time’.
  4. Once you have chosen your appointment(s) and clicked ‘Select and continue’ you’ll be directed to the confirmation page. Check the boxes and click CONTINUE TO PAYMENT.
  5. You’ll be taken to the Checkout page. No additional payment is required as you’re using your membership class credits.
  6. Press confirm and your booking(s) is complete! I look forward to welcoming you to class!
