Ongoing Support, Tailored to You

10 personalised wellbeing sessions across the year, tailored to what you need each time — with £10 off every session and the option to spread the cost monthly.

Regular sessions give you dedicated time throughout the year to look after your body and wellbeing, rather than waiting until tension, stress or discomfort builds up. Your subscription includes 10 sessions to use across the year, with £10 off each appointment.

  • 10 Sessions a year View Wellbeing Subscriptions · choose 60 or 90 minutes
  • £10 Off every session Read the terms and conditions

Tailored each time

Each session is tailored to what you need on the day. Your appointment might combine therapeutic massage with breath work, movement, self-myofascial release or guided relaxation, or you may prefer to use your session for Clinical & Sports Massage or Hot Stones Massage.

How Regular Wellbeing Sessions Can Help

  • Reduce muscular tension and everyday aches
  • Support relaxation and stress management
  • Maintain comfortable movement and mobility
  • Create regular time to slow down and reconnect with your body
  • Support better rest and overall wellbeing
  • Address changing needs before tension or discomfort builds up

Why Choose the 90-Minute Subscription?

The 90-minute option gives us more time to work at an unhurried pace and combine different approaches within one session.

It’s particularly suited to you if you’d like to explore breath work, movement, self-myofascial release or guided relaxation alongside hands-on treatment.

Your Subscription Benefits

  • 10 personalised sessions across the year
  • £10 saving on every session
  • Priority booking
  • Flexible monthly payments
  • Choice of 60- or 90-minute sessions
  • Treatment tailored to your needs each time
  • Cancel anytime

Ready to Make Your Wellbeing a Priority?

Choose the subscription that suits you best and make regular, personalised wellbeing support part of your year.

Wellbeing Subscribers Booking

Use your unique subscription code each time you book one of your 10 wellbeing sessions.

The easiest way to book is to use the Schedule link in the Order Receipt emailed to you when you purchased your subscription. You can also book through the usual booking links on the Move with Ease website and enter your unique subscription code at checkout.

If you need any help booking your sessions or using your subscription code, please email me at info@movewithease.org.uk and I’ll be happy to help.
