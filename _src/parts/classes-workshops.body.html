Release Stress & Tension with TRE®

A gentle introduction to TRE® (Tension & Trauma Releasing Exercises®). You’ll be guided through the exercises and explore your body’s natural tremor response at a comfortable pace, with an emphasis on choice, self-regulation and feeling in control. You’ll learn how to start, slow, pause and stop the tremors, giving you the foundations to continue practising TRE® safely at home.

Deep Rest & Renewal

Feeling overwhelmed, tense, overheated, or simply in need of a reset? A nourishing two-hour workshop in Paddock Wood, Kent, designed to soothe your nervous system and help you reconnect with yourself.

Through a carefully curated blend of cooling breathwork, meditation, restorative yoga, self-massage with soft massage balls, and guided hypnorelaxation, you'll be gently guided into a state of deep rest. Each practice is designed to ease physical tension, quiet a busy mind, and leave you feeling calmer, lighter, and deeply renewed.

Ideal if you're looking to reduce stress, improve sleep, ease persistent tension, support chronic pain recovery, or simply create space to pause and restore.
