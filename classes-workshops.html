Classes & Workshops

Move with Ease classes and workshops support the nervous system through gentle movement, breath work, relaxation, and body-based practices.

Sessions are designed to help ease persistent pain, reduce stress and anxiety, and build a greater sense of safety. Weekly classes run in Paddock Wood and Laddingford, with small-group workshops through the year.

Yoga & Fascia Movement

Yoga and fascia-focused movement classes in Paddock Wood — also available for private bookings. Each class combines traditional yoga with fascia-focused movement, mobility, self-myofascial release using soft massage balls, breath work, mindfulness and relaxation.

This is a friendly, supportive members-only class with a maximum of 15 people. If you’re interested in joining, please get in touch to check availability.

Release Stress & Tension with TRE®

A gentle introduction to TRE® (Tension & Trauma Releasing Exercises®). You’ll be guided through the exercises and explore your body’s natural tremor response at a comfortable pace, with an emphasis on choice, self-regulation and feeling in control. You’ll learn how to start, slow, pause and stop the tremors, giving you the foundations to continue practising TRE® safely at home.

Deep Rest & Renewal

Feeling overwhelmed, tense, overheated, or simply in need of a reset? A nourishing two-hour workshop in Paddock Wood, Kent, designed to soothe your nervous system and help you reconnect with yourself.

Through a carefully curated blend of cooling breathwork, meditation, restorative yoga, self-massage with soft massage balls, and guided hypnorelaxation, you'll be gently guided into a state of deep rest. Each practice is designed to ease physical tension, quiet a busy mind, and leave you feeling calmer, lighter, and deeply renewed.

Ideal if you're looking to reduce stress, improve sleep, ease persistent tension, support chronic pain recovery, or simply create space to pause and restore.

Deep Rest & Renewal Evening with Sound & Gong

Take an evening just for you — a chance to pause, reset, and restore balance before the festive season. This nourishing 2.5-hour workshop combines gentle yoga movement, meditation, breath work, with a deeply immersive Sound & Gong Bath, designed to calm your nervous system and bring harmony to body, mind, and soul. In collaboration with Harmony in Sound.

Perfect if you’re looking to ease stress, find calm, or simply step out of the December busyness and return home to yourself. Sovereign Hall is the first hall off the foyer at Paddock Wood Community Centre. There is ample free off-road parking available at the venue.

Contraindications — Pregnancy: only suitable during the second trimester. Not recommended in the first or third trimester.

New Year Renewal — Strength & Ease
