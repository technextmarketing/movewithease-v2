Where I work

Based at my private clinic in Laddingford, I welcome clients from Maidstone, Tonbridge, Paddock Wood and across Kent. I also practise at The Spring Rooms in Tunbridge Wells on Tuesday afternoons and Friday mornings. Many sessions are also available online via Zoom, so you can work with me wherever you are.
