## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS with no runtime dependencies. Pages are assembled from `_src/parts/` by `python _src/build.py` (Python 3, standard library only). Hostable next to the existing Weebly site, on GitHub Pages, or on any static host.

## Users

- **Primary: the person in pain, anxious or stressed** (or the partner, parent or friend looking on their behalf) in west Kent — Maidstone, Tonbridge, Paddock Wood, Tunbridge Wells — or anywhere online. Usually on a phone. Job: understand what Caroline offers, whether it fits their situation, what it costs and where it happens, then book a free discovery call or a session.
- **Secondary: the returning client.** Wants to book quickly, use a subscription or membership code, find class dates, or check hours. Job: reach the right Acuity booking link in one or two taps.
- **Tertiary: the gift buyer and the enquirer.** Buys a voucher, or asks about a bespoke amount, a workshop date, a members-only class place or an appointment outside clinic hours.

## Product Purpose

Present Move with Ease — Caroline’s integrative wellbeing and chronic pain recovery practice — clearly enough that a first-time visitor knows within seconds what this is, who it is for, what it costs and how to start. Route every booking to the existing Acuity Scheduling links, every question to the enquiry form or info@movewithease.org.uk, and every voucher purchase to the Acuity catalogue. Success is a booked discovery call or session, a completed purchase, or a well-formed enquiry Caroline can answer in one reply.

## Positioning

Not a spa. One practitioner, Caroline Miller — a former nurse, Clinical & Sports Massage Therapist (Jing Method® BTEC Level 6), Pain Reprocessing Therapy® practitioner, certified TRE® provider, Buteyko breathing coach, yoga and FAMO fascia movement teacher and clinical hypnotherapist — working from a private garden clinic in Laddingford, Kent, at The Spring Rooms in Tunbridge Wells for massage, at Paddock Wood Community Centre for classes and workshops, and online via Zoom. The mechanism that unites the offer: helping an overprotective nervous system learn safety, through hands-on, breath, movement and mind-body approaches tailored to the person.

## Product Principles

1. Faithful copy: every claim, price and date on the site comes from the live site.
2. Answer the first-visit questions in the first viewport: what, for whom, where, what it costs, how to start.
3. One practitioner, one voice: first person, unhurried, clinical trust over spa gloss.
4. Booking is never more than one tap away; the enquiry form is one tap behind it.
5. Nothing invented: no stock people, no made-up reviews or numbers; past dates are labelled as past.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visitors may be in pain, tired or stressed, and may use larger text. Target WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body contrast in both colour schemes, 44px touch targets, visible focus, reduced-motion respected, no autoplaying media, readable at 200% zoom, keyboard-operable navigation and form.
