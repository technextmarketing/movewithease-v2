Documentaries & podcasts on Pain Reprocessing Therapy

Discover the groundbreaking findings of the Pain Reprocessing Therapy study in the compelling documentary, ‘Pain Brain’. Dive into the transformative journey of chronic back pain patients and explore the remarkable results of this groundbreaking research.

Emerging neuroscience reveals that our brains play a crucial role, creating the pain we feel through predictive processing. It's not just about the body part; it's a signal from the brain, highlighting areas needing attention. Dr. Rangan Chatterjee has welcomed Howard Schubiner, PRT Medical Advisor, to his podcast twice! And in his 2026 Channel 4 Documentary ‘Live Well with the Drug-Free Doctor’ he celebrates the transformational benefits of PRT for chronic pain recovery!
