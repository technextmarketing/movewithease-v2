Cupping Therapy

A focused, therapeutic technique to release tension, boost circulation, and support recovery.

Cupping therapy is a deeply effective hands-on technique that uses glass cups to create gentle suction on the skin. It’s a modern application of an ancient therapy, now used widely in clinical massage, sports recovery, and chronic pain support.

What is Cupping Therapy?

It reduces pain and muscular tension, promoting glide in fascia and soft tissue structures, thereby increasing joint range and movement ease. It reduces DOMS (delayed onset muscle soreness) after exercise—so you can return to your training more quickly.

Why Choose Cupping?

  • Reduces pain and eases muscular tension
  • Promotes circulation and lymphatic flow
  • Releases fascial adhesions and scar tissue
  • Soothes the nervous system
  • Speeds recovery from exercise or injury

Whether you’re dealing with a sore back, post-workout fatigue, or ongoing tightness, cupping can help shift stagnation and create space for your body to rebalance and heal.

What to Expect in a 30-Minute Session

After a brief check-in, I’ll tailor your session based on your goals. You’ll receive:

  • Targeted static cupping (cups remain in place)
  • Glide cupping to mobilise fascia and improve movement
  • Optional fire cupping or flash cupping depending on your needs and comfort

Cupping is generally a pain-free experience. Some pink or purple marks—called piritha—may appear afterwards. These are not bruises, as the muscle tissue isn’t damaged. Instead, they’re a natural response as blood flow increases beneath the skin and fascia releases.

One of the benefits of using glass cups is the ability to clearly observe what's happening under the skin. This allows me to monitor the tissue’s response, adjust the pressure, and watch for any adverse reactions—making the treatment both safe and effective.
