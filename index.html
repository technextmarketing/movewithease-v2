Hello, I’m Caroline

I’m an integrative wellbeing practitioner specialising in chronic pain recovery, nervous system regulation and mind-body approaches, based in Laddingford, Kent.

My work brings together clinical hypnotherapy, Pain Reprocessing Therapy, clinical & sports massage, breath work, mindfulness and movement-based approaches. Rather than following a one-size-fits-all method, I draw on different approaches according to you, what you’re experiencing and what feels most helpful.

I work with people experiencing persistent pain, anxiety, stress, tension and other mind-body symptoms, supporting you to understand the patterns that may be contributing to symptoms or making them harder to shift.

Sessions are collaborative and tailored to you, with an emphasis on helping you feel more at ease, build confidence in your own capacity for change and move towards the things that matter to you.

More about Caroline

Understanding chronic pain

In this short video, I explain how pain can persist even after tissues have healed, and how changing the way the brain interprets threat and safety can help change the pain experience.

For some people, chronic pain can be influenced by an overprotective nervous system that continues to produce pain even when the body is no longer in danger. The encouraging news is that these pain pathways can change. By understanding pain differently and gradually helping the brain and nervous system learn safety, recovery may be possible.
