Live with Ease — Relaxation Class

A calming evening class combining meditation, mindfulness, breathing, and relaxation techniques to ease stress, anxiety, and pain — offering deep rest and renewal.

Free class to new customers! Wednesday 6–7pm, Laddingford, Kent or online via Zoom. Only 6 spaces are available in person at Move with Ease HQ, Laddingford.

  • £10 Pay-as-you-go class Wednesday 6–7pm · Laddingford or Zoom · book here
  • £28 Or use your classes membership Press the pay-as-you-go button and add your unique membership code so you don’t pay twice

What the class offers

Empower yourself with breathing, mindfulness, meditation, Pain Reprocessing Therapy™ techniques and psychoeducation that:

  • Regulate emotions and the nervous system
  • Stimulate the vagus nerve
  • Build interoceptive and somatic awareness
  • Enhance respiratory function and breathing efficiency
  • Experience greater calm, health and overall well-being
  • Improved pain management
  • Empower and promote self-autonomy
  • Helping you to live with ease

Knowledge is not power, it's potential power. It only becomes power when you use it

Jim Kwik

Each class aims to empower participants with knowledge and a skill set to promote self-autonomy and recovery from anxiety, stress and persistent pain. The overall goal though is to live with ease.

Inside each class

Breathing techniques

Yoga pranayama, Buteyko and Oxygen Advantage™ breathing techniques.

Meditation

Formal seated meditation and mindfulness techniques from the Mindfulness Now™ program composing of mindfulness-based-stress-reduction (MBSR) and mindfulness-based-cognitive-therapy (MBCT).

Pain Reprocessing Therapy™

Somatic meditation techniques that teach you to embrace discomfort and painful physical sensations. You don’t push away comfortable sensations so learn to lean into pain with a curious, open mind.

Lunchtime class — Chronic Pain Recovery & Stress Reduction

Living with persistent pain, anxiety, or stress can feel exhausting — but things can change. This weekly donation-based class offers a supportive space to calm your nervous system, ease tension, and explore proven techniques to retrain the brain’s pain pathways.

What to Expect

Each 60-minute session includes gentle, guided practices from:

  • Mindfulness – to quiet busy thoughts and bring a sense of calm
  • Functional breathing – to restore balance and reduce anxiety
  • Pain Reprocessing Therapy – science-based tools to shift how the brain interprets pain signals

Chairs are available, or you can sit/lie on the floor if you prefer. Please bring a mat, cushion, blanket, water, and a pen and notebook for short reflections. Plenty of free parking is available at the venue.
